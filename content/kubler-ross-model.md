Also known as the "five stages of grief". Described in the book *On Death and Dying* (1969). The stages need not occur completely, nor in the given order.

1. **Denial** — shock and rejection of reality.
2. **Anger** — frustration, "why me?".
3. **Bargaining** — trying to avert or postpone the inevitable.
4. **Depression** — sadness and awareness of the loss.
5. **Acceptance** — coming to terms with the situation.
