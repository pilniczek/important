Turns long-form videos and podcasts into interactive argument maps. Every claim is tagged by epistemic status (fact / opinion / speculation) and linked back to its exact timestamp in the source. The same transcript is viewable through ten "lenses" — argument topology, blindspots, steelman, timeline, principles, implications, and more.

Useful when you want to interrogate a talk, not skim a summary. Paid (monthly subscription or one-time credit packs).
