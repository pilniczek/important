**Grill with Docs** question you until you reach *shared understanding*.

- **Know which questions are grillable.** Talk through low-fidelity ones (a route's URL); for high-fidelity ones (UI feel) [[handoff-skill|hand off to a prototype]] and bring learnings back.
- **Keep scope small.** Grill one bounded thing so you stay out of the dumb zone (~120k tokens). Break big scope into smaller scopes first.
- **Lead the conversation** - steer actively, but stop talking and build when words can't answer.
- **Don't waste the session.** Implement off the decisions, or capture them in a handoff/PRD - don't clear context cold.
- **Grill with a smart model** (it leans on parametric knowledge); implementation can run cheaper.
- **Run ~2 sessions in parallel** to roughly double planning throughput.
